Privacy Policy for X Water
Effective Date: July 24, 2026
Last Updated: July 24, 2026
Developer: Jayveen
1. Introduction
X Water (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a water reminder and tracking application for iOS and Apple Watch developed by Jayveen. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the X Water app.
By using X Water, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
2. Information We Collect
X Water is designed to be simple and privacy-focused. We collect only the minimum information needed for the app to function:
•  Water intake data you enter (amounts, times, and daily goals you set).
•  App preferences and settings (reminder times, units, theme choices, etc.).
•  Device permissions you grant (such as notifications for reminders).
We do not collect:
•  Your name, email address, or contact details
•  Location data
•  Health data from Apple Health / HealthKit (unless you explicitly enable any future integration)
•  Advertising identifiers
•  Analytics or usage tracking beyond what is strictly required for basic app function
All water tracking data stays on your device unless you choose to use iCloud (if enabled in your device settings).
3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide solely to:
•  Display your water intake progress
•  Send the reminders you have configured
•  Remember your preferences
•  Improve the reliability of the app on your device
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.
4. Data Storage and Security
•  Water logs and settings are stored locally on your device.
•  If iCloud is enabled on your Apple ID, some data may sync across your devices via Apple’s iCloud service (subject to Apple’s privacy policy).
•  We do not operate our own servers that store your personal water data.
You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data in iOS Settings.
5. Third-Party Services
X Water does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social media SDKs.
The only external services involved are those provided by Apple (notifications, iCloud, App Store). Those services are governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
6. Children’s Privacy
X Water does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not require account creation or personal data.
7. Your Rights and Choices
You can:
•  Change or delete your water logs inside the app
•  Turn reminders on or off at any time
•  Revoke notification permission in iOS Settings
•  Delete the app to remove all associated data
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
