Privacy Policy for X Water
Effective Date: July 25, 2026
Last Updated: September 21, 2026
Developer: Jayveen
Introduction
X Water (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a water reminder and tracking application for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch developed by Jayveen. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the X Water app. By using X Water, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
Information We Collect
X Water collects only the minimum information needed for the app to work:
•  Water intake amounts and times that you enter
•  Your daily goal and app preferences (units, reminder settings, theme, and similar settings)
•  Notification permission, if you enable reminders
•  A yes/no Premium status on your device if you buy Premium
Apple Health
If you turn on “Sync with Apple Health”, the app will write your water intake (dietary water) to Apple Health. We only write water data. We do not read other health data from Apple Health unless you allow it.
Purchases
Premium is handled by Apple. We do not get your card details. We only keep a yes/no Premium status on your device.
We do not collect:
•  Your name, email, or contact details through the app
•  Location data
•  Advertising identifiers
•  Analytics or tracking data
•  Camera photos or camera data
How We Use Your Information
We use the information only to:
•  Show your daily water progress
•  Send the reminders you set
•  Save your preferences
•  Optionally log water to Apple Health
•  Unlock Premium features on your device
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for advertising or marketing.
Data Storage
All water logs and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData. If you enable iCloud, some data may sync across your devices through Apple’s iCloud service. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal water data.
Third-Party Services
The only external services used are provided by Apple (Notifications, HealthKit, iCloud, App Store, and In-App Purchases). These are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
Children’s Privacy
X Water does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not require an account.
Your Choices
You can:
•  Turn Apple Health sync on or off at any time in Settings
•  Turn reminders on or off
•  Restore or manage Premium through Apple
•  Delete all app data by deleting the app
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted with an updated date.
